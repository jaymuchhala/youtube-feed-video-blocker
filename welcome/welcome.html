You're all set

Feed Video/Image Blocker for YouTube

  • Thumbnails stay hidden

    Thumbnails, hover previews and post images in your feeds are collapsed on sight, and Shorts shelves are gone.

  • Reveal what you want

    Click the eye next to a video's ⋮ menu to show its thumbnail, and again to hide it.

  • Fold away comments

    On a video's page, click the scroll next to "Sort by" to hide the comments, and again to bring them back.

  • Only media is touched

    Titles, channels and the video you're watching are left as they are. Nothing leaves your browser.
